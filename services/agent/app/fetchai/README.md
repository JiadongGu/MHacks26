# Pulse Health Agent

![tag:innovationlab](https://img.shields.io/badge/innovationlab-3D8BD3)
![tag:hackathon](https://img.shields.io/badge/hackathon-5F43F1)

Pulse is a personal health agent. It reads your wearable vitals (Fitbit, Apple Watch), builds a digital twin from your
health record, and acts on what it sees. Chat with it here the same way you do over iMessage.

## What you can ask
- **Status**: "how am I doing today?" Latest heart rate, steps, and any open alerts.
- **Sleep summary**: "how did I sleep?" Last night and the 3-night trend against your goal.
- **Steps and goals**: "how am I doing on steps this week?"
- **Block sleep on my calendar**: "block sleep tonight". Pulse proposes a time on your Google Calendar and only
  writes the event after you reply YES.
- **Why did you alert me?** Pulse explains its last alert.
